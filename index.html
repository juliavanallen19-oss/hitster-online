📖 How to play
  • Draw a card — scan the QR code with your phone to listen to the song.
  • Place it — tap a slot on your timeline where you think the song belongs chronologically (oldest on the left, newest on the right).
  • Correct? The card stays. Wrong? It's discarded.
  • Bonus token ✪ — guess both the artist and song title correctly to earn an extra token.
  • Spend tokens — Skip a card (1✪), attempt a HITSTER! steal (1✪), or buy a guaranteed placement without a year check (3✪). Max 5 tokens per player.
  • HITSTER! steal — after the active player chooses a slot, one challenger can pay 1✪ to pick a different slot on the active player's timeline. If the challenger is right and the active player is wrong, the challenger steals the card.
  • Win — first to reach the goal number of cards wins!
