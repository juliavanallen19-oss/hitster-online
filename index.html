Hitster Online

Build your timeline. Guess the year. Win the game.

? New here? Read How to Play before starting

Goal

  • Be the first player to collect the target number of cards on your personal timeline. The default is 10 cards — you can change this before starting.

Setup

  • Each player receives one starter card already placed on their timeline as a reference point.
  • Everyone also receives starting tokens ✪ depending on the chosen mode (see Modes below).
  • The remaining songs are shuffled into a shared deck. One player goes first — chosen in setup.

A Turn

  • 1. Listen — a QR code appears. Scan it with your phone to open the song on Spotify and listen.
  • 2. Place — tap a slot on your timeline where you think the song belongs chronologically. Your timeline runs oldest → newest, left to right. Slots appear between and around your existing cards.
  • 3. Name guess (optional) — before revealing the answer, you can try to name the artist and/or song title for a bonus token ✪. For songs with featured artists, naming just the main artist is enough. See mode rules for what counts.
  • 4. Submit — hit Submit to lock in your placement and name guess. The card flips to reveal the year, artist, and title.
  • 5. Result — correct placement means the card stays on your timeline. Wrong placement means the card is discarded. Either way, the turn passes to the next player.

Tokens ✪

  • Earning tokens — guess the name correctly during your turn to earn +1 token. For featured songs, the main artist alone counts (e.g. "Eminem" for Eminem ft. Rihanna). Tokens are capped at 5 per player.
  • Skip (1 ✪) — discard the current card and draw a fresh one instead. Useful if you have no idea about the song.
  • HITSTER! Challenge (1 ✪) — before the active player submits, any other player can pay 1 token to challenge. See the Challenge section below.
  • Buy Placement (3 ✪) — spend 3 tokens to let the game place the card automatically in the correct chronological position. No guessing, no risk — guaranteed card.

HITSTER! Challenge

  • After the active player has chosen their slot but before they submit, any other player may pay 1 token to challenge.
  • The challenger picks a different slot on the active player's timeline — betting the active player is wrong and they know the right position.
  • Active player correct — they keep the card. The challenger loses their token.
  • Challenger correct, active player wrong — the challenger wins the card, automatically added to their own timeline.
  • Both wrong — the card is discarded. The challenger already lost their token.
  • Only one challenge attempt is allowed per turn.
  • PRO mode only: the challenger must also name the song correctly to win the challenge. If they win, their token is returned.

Modes

  • Original — the classic experience. Start with 2 tokens. Place the card correctly to keep it. Guess both artist and title correctly for a bonus token. The steal works on position alone.
  • Chill — more relaxed. Start with 3 tokens. Same placement rules as Original. For the bonus token, guessing either the artist or the title is enough — you don't need both. Empty fields don't count as wrong.
  • PRO — hardest mode. Start with 5 tokens. To keep a card you must get the placement and the name (artist + title) both right. Getting only one of the two is not enough. For steals, the challenger must also name correctly to win — but if they do, their token is returned to them.

Winning

  • Normal win — the first player to reach the target number of cards on their timeline wins immediately.
  • Deck runs out — if the song deck is empty and no one has hit the target yet, the player with the most cards wins. If still tied, the player with the most tokens wins. If still tied, all tied players share the victory.

Good to know

  • Songs from the same year can be placed in any order relative to each other — both slots are accepted.
  • If the active player typed the song name with a small typo, any other player can tap "Mark as correct" to still award the bonus token.

Players

Add 2 to 6 players.
